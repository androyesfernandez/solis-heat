**Solis Heat** is a complete open-world crime game that runs entirely in the browser — vanilla JavaScript, no libraries, no assets, no network. You are Rafi Solano, back in the sun-bleached city of Marina Solis: a 3600×2700 grid of seven avenues and five streets between the sea and the east side, with a beach and promenade, palm trees, some four dozen named pastel buildings and nine landmarks — Mercado Central, the Solis Chop Shop, the Docks, Clinica Palma, Sunset Pawn, the Faro de Solis lighthouse, Solis Airfield, the PD Precinct and the Crash Pad. Walk it on foot or steal any of the parked cars (six original models, from the humble Solis Burro to the Miramar Luxe), cut through living traffic, and work for fixer Tia Carmen through a four-mission story — a first delivery, a timed run under a two-star ambush, a pawn-shop hold-up and escape, and a final run with a three-star heat floor — followed by endless generated side jobs. A full wanted system tracks heat star by star: Solis PD Interceptors pursue and deploy officers who will bust you if they pin you, and open fire at three stars. Mouse-aim pistol combat, a cash-and-respect economy, BUSTED and TAKEN DOWN outcomes with respawns at the Precinct and the Clinica, a live minimap, localStorage saves, and a fully synthesised WebAudio soundtrack of engines and sirens round it out. Everything — city, characters, cars, story — is original to this entry.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser — or simply open `game/index.html` directly from disk. Controls: WASD or arrow keys to move and drive, F or E to enter and exit cars, mouse to aim and click to shoot, K to save, L to load, P or Esc to pause.
